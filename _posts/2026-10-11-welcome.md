This is a sample post. I plan to use this space for short, one-page notes on topics in theoretical physics: matrix models, holography, black holes and whatever else I am learning about.

Equations are rendered with MathJax. Inline math looks like this: the Schwarzian action is $$ S = -C \int d\tau \, \{ f(\tau), \tau \} $$, and display math goes on its own lines:

$$
Z(\beta) = \int_0^\infty dE \, \rho(E) \, e^{-\beta E}, \qquad
\rho(E) \sim e^{S_0} \sinh\left(2\pi\sqrt{2CE}\right).
$$
