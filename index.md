I am a theoretical physicist working on quantum gravity, holography and string theory. My research uses tools from matrix models, random matrix theory and conformal field theory to study black holes and the quantum structure of spacetime.

Topics I have worked on include:

- **Matrix models and black holes:** lattice bosonization of the double scaled c=1 matrix model, and the role of global symmetry and gauging in matrix models and black hole cohomologies.
- **SYK models and holography:** gravitational collapse in SYK models, holographic duals of charged SYK from 3D gravity and Chern-Simons theory, and spectral form factors in random matrix theories.
- **Black hole evaporation and information loss:** microscopic models of black hole evaporation in two dimensions.
- **Conformal field theory and the bootstrap:** boundary Liouville CFT in four dimensions, and the QCD worldsheet axion from the bootstrap.
- **Quantum quenches:** thermalization to generalized Gibbs ensembles in arbitrary dimensions.

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the Mandelstam Institute for Theoretical Physics, University of the Witwatersrand, Johannesburg. Before that I was a postdoctoral researcher at Tel Aviv University, and I did my Ph.D. at the Tata Institute of Fundamental Research, Mumbai.
